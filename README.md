# Airple Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Airple Construction Pte Ltd provides aircon servicing, installation, maintenance, and repairs for homes and businesses across Singapore. The proposed homepage puts Airple’s digital service experience up front and helps visitors compare services and book.

## Main Improvements

1. **Show how the digital service works.** Visitors have to look for the features that distinguish Airple. A section near the top of the homepage would use genuine app screenshots to show how customers book, track technician arrival, view service records, and approve repairs.
2. **Make pricing and company information consistent.** Service cards would put starting prices, inclusions, package terms, additional charges, and applicable GST in a consistent format. The redesign would use verified review figures and credentials, genuine testimonials, and a confirmed customer-facing address and contact details.
3. **Make booking easier from any service.** A prominent **Book a Service** button, direct enquiry links on service and pricing cards, and persistent mobile Call and WhatsApp buttons would give visitors a shorter path to booking. The enquiry form would ask for service type, unit quantity, and postal code. App downloads and online booking would each have a clearly labelled action.

## Design Improvements

App screenshots would illustrate the book, track, and manage steps. Consistent card layouts would make prices easier to compare, while distinct booking and download buttons would help visitors choose how to proceed.
